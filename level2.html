DR. A.P.J. ABDUL KALAM

ABOUT

Dr. A.P.J Abdul kalam was a great scientist and the 11th president of India.He was born on 15th october 1931 in Rameswaram,Tamil Nadu.

He played an important role in India's space and Missile programmes. He was also a great teacher and inspired millions of students to follow their dreams.

His Achievements

Dr. kalam worked with ISRO and DRDO and contributed to India's space and defence programmes. He become the president of india in 2002 and received the Bharat Ratan,India's civilian award.
